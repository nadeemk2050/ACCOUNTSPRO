3 · Automatic Rules & Validation

Date and Amount are mandatory; amount must be greater than 0.
Paid To must exist in masters — otherwise the row waits in Solution Centre until created.
Paid From must be a Cash / Bank ledger of the company.
Duplicate voucher numbers already in the system are quarantined (auto-renumber or skip).
Same Voucher No + Date = one voucher with multiple receivers (multi-split payout).
Foreign currency rows need a valid exchange rate.
Dates inside the locked period are rejected.
XML files: Tally “Vouchers” export (Data Interchange) also accepted.
Tip: Inside the app open Management Hub → Import Payment → Instructions & Templates and press Download Standard (.xlsx) for a ready-made file with these exact headers.
