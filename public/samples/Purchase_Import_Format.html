3 · Automatic Rules & Validation

Date, Supplier, Item, Qty and Rate are mandatory.
Party and every item must exist in masters — missing ones appear in Solution Centre.
Rows sharing the same Voucher No + Date become one voucher with multiple item lines.
Amount can be left empty — Qty × Rate is used. Tax % or Tax Amount is optional.
Duplicate voucher numbers already in the system are quarantined (auto-renumber or skip).
Dates inside the locked period are rejected.
Stock is posted automatically for every item line (opening / closing quantities update).
Tally XML purchase vouchers are accepted on the same screen.
